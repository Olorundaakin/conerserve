Community Guidelines

Building a trusted, respectful CornerServe community.

Our Commitment

CornerServe works because people trust each other. These guidelines exist to keep that trust strong — for customers looking for help, and for providers building their reputation.

For Everyone

  • Be honest. Provide accurate information about yourself and your needs or services.
  • Be respectful. Treat every person you interact with on CornerServe with courtesy.
  • Communicate clearly. Misunderstandings often come from unclear expectations — be specific.
  • Report problems. If something feels wrong, let us know rather than handling it publicly or aggressively.

For Customers

  • Describe your service needs as clearly and accurately as possible.
  • Respect a provider's time — communicate promptly and show up for scheduled services.
  • Leave honest reviews based on your real experience.
  • Treat providers as professionals, not employees.

For Providers

  • Only list services you are genuinely qualified and equipped to provide.
  • Honor the pricing and availability shown on your profile.
  • Communicate promptly and professionally with customers.
  • Deliver the quality of work customers should reasonably expect.

What's Not Allowed

  • Harassment, threats, or abusive behavior toward any user
  • Discrimination based on race, religion, gender, ethnicity, or any other protected status
  • Fake reviews, fake profiles, or misrepresenting your identity
  • Attempting to take payments or communication outside the platform to avoid verification and accountability
  • Any illegal activity conducted through or facilitated by CornerServe

How We Handle Violations

Reports of guideline violations are reviewed on a case-by-case basis. Depending on severity, this may result in a warning, temporary suspension, or permanent removal from CornerServe.

Reporting a Concern

If you experience or witness behavior that goes against these guidelines, please reach out through our Contact page so our team can look into it.
