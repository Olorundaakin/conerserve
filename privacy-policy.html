Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

CornerServe ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account or use CornerServe, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location / state
  • Account password (stored securely, never in plain text)
  • Whether you're using CornerServe to find services, offer services, or both

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Connect customers with relevant service providers
  • Verify provider identities to keep the platform trustworthy
  • Communicate with you about your account or activity on CornerServe
  • Improve and maintain the CornerServe platform

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. Limited information may be shared in the following cases:

  • Between customers and providers when a service is requested, to facilitate contact
  • With service providers we use to operate CornerServe (such as hosting or payment processing), only as needed
  • When required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your personal information at any time. To make such a request, please contact us using the details on our Contact page.

7. Children's Privacy

CornerServe is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page.
